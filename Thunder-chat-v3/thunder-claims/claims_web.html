Help Topics

Finding things. Pick Patients, Claims, Insurers or Providers on the left. Type in the box under any column to filter it; a % matches anywhere (%tom finds JONES, TOM). Click a heading to sort, shift-click to sort by more than one. Drag a heading up into the grey band to group by it. Right-click a heading for more, including Export to Excel.

Opening records. Click a row to see it in the detail pane below the list; double-click (or press Enter) to open it in a tab. Several can be open at once. A * on a tab means it has unsaved changes.

Claims. Pick the patient, and the claim fills in from the patient record. Click a date on the calendars to add a service line. Diagnosis codes can be typed without the decimal point. Scrub checks the claim; it also runs as you type. Print makes a CMS-1500-layout copy and marks the claim Submitted.

Pink rows are claims (or patients with claims) that have been submitted and still have money owed.

Alt-S saves and closes, as in EZClaim.

Nothing is ever sent to a payer from here. Everything is stored encrypted on Main. Synthetic patients only for now.
